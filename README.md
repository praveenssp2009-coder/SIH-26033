## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.  
• Provides logistics support.  
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.  
• Lower prices for consumers.  
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution

Develop an AI-powered digital marketplace that directly connects farmers, FPOs, consumers, and bulk buyers. Farmers can list their products, update prices, and sell directly without depending on multiple intermediaries. Consumers can purchase fresh agricultural products at affordable prices. The platform also provides transparent pricing, order management, and delivery tracking.

## Technical Approach

• **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript.  
• **Backend:** Python and Django.  
• **Database:** MySQL.  
• **AI/ML:** Python and Scikit-learn for demand forecasting.  
• **Maps Integration:** Google Maps API for route optimization.  
• **Payment Integration:** UPI and online payment gateways.

**Implementation Methodology:**

1. Farmers register and upload their available products.
2. Consumers and bulk buyers browse products and place orders.
3. The platform connects buyers directly with farmers.
4. AI analyses market demand and predicts future requirements.
5. Logistics support helps arrange product transportation.
6. Buyers receive their products and farmers receive payments.

## Feasibility and Viability

• The solution can be developed using affordable and easily available technologies.  
• Farmers can access the platform through smartphones and computers.  
• A simple interface with regional language support makes it easier to use.  
• Direct transactions reduce dependency on intermediaries.  
• Partnerships with local transport providers can support product delivery.  
• AI-based demand forecasting helps reduce excess production and food wastage.  
• The platform can generate revenue through a small service fee on successful transactions.

## Impact and Benefits

• **Farmers:** Increased earnings through direct sales and reduced intermediary costs.  
• **Consumers:** Access to fresh agricultural products at affordable prices.  
• **Economic Impact:** Reduced supply chain expenses and improved market efficiency.  
• **Social Impact:** Supports farmers and rural communities through digital technology.  
• **Environmental Impact:** Reduces food wastage and improves transportation efficiency.  
• **Rural Development:** Encourages digital adoption and creates new employment opportunities.
